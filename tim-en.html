Our Team

The PsySocFear-F project brings together researchers and professionals from different fields with expertise relevant to the study of psychosocial factors associated with fear of cancer recurrence.

Project Leadership

Associate Professor Lovorka Brajković, PhD

Principal Investigator

Faculty of Croatian Studies, University of Zagreb

Research Team

Psychology and Psycho-Oncology

Researchers and professionals with expertise in psychology and psycho-oncology contribute to the understanding of psychological experiences associated with cancer and life after treatment.

Research Methodology

The project includes expertise in research methodology and the design and analysis of data relevant to the research questions.

Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Researchers and professionals from relevant fields contribute to an interdisciplinary approach to the project's research objectives.

A Collaborative Approach

The interdisciplinary nature of the PsySocFear-F project enables different perspectives and areas of expertise to be brought together in the study of fear of cancer recurrence.

Through collaboration, the project aims to better understand the psychological and social needs of women after cancer treatment and to contribute to the development of appropriate forms of psychological support.
