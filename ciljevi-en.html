Project Objectives

The main objective of the PsySocFear-F project is to investigate psychosocial factors associated with fear of cancer recurrence among women diagnosed with breast and gynecological cancers.

Main Objective

The project aims to gain a better understanding of the psychological and social factors that may contribute to the development and maintenance of fear of cancer recurrence after the completion of cancer treatment.

Specific Objectives

Understanding Fear of Cancer Recurrence

To investigate the experiences of women living after cancer treatment and better understand the nature and intensity of fear of cancer recurrence.

Identifying Psychosocial Factors

To identify psychological and social factors associated with fear of cancer recurrence and examine their relationship with psychological well-being and quality of life.

Understanding Individual Experiences

To explore the personal experiences and needs of women following cancer treatment, including the challenges they may face in everyday life.

Developing Psychological Support

To contribute to the development of appropriate forms of psychological support for women living after cancer treatment.

Educational Materials

To provide a basis for the development of educational materials and resources for patients, their families and relevant professionals.

Recommendations for Practice

To contribute to the development of recommendations relevant to professional practice and to improve the recognition of psychosocial needs after cancer treatment.

Expected Contribution

The findings of the PsySocFear-F project are expected to contribute to a better understanding of fear of cancer recurrence and the psychosocial needs of women after cancer treatment.

The results may provide a foundation for improving psychological support, developing educational resources and strengthening professional practice in the field of psycho-oncology.
