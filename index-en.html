Psychosocial factors as predictors of fear of cancer recurrence in women diagnosed with breast and gynecological cancers

PsySocFear-F is a research project focused on better understanding psychosocial factors associated with fear of cancer recurrence among women after cancer treatment.

Learn more about the project

About the Project

Full project title Psychosocial factors as predictors of fear of cancer recurrence in women diagnosed with breast and gynecological cancers
Acronym PsySocFear-F
Project coordinator Faculty of Croatian Studies, University of Zagreb
Principal Investigator Associate Professor Lovorka Brajković, PhD
Project duration 2025 – 2029
Research area Psychology, psycho-oncology and psychosocial aspects of life after cancer treatment

Why is this research important?

Completing cancer treatment does not necessarily mean the end of the psychological challenges associated with the disease. Fear of a possible recurrence can remain an important part of life after treatment.

The PsySocFear-F project focuses on investigating psychological and social factors that may be associated with fear of cancer recurrence among women diagnosed with breast and gynecological cancers.

A better understanding of these experiences may contribute to improved recognition of the needs of people after cancer treatment and to the development of appropriate forms of psychological support.

Project objectives

Research Areas

Psychological Factors

Research into psychological factors associated with fear of cancer recurrence and psychological adjustment after treatment.

Social Factors

Research into the role of social support and other factors within people's social and living environments.

Psychological Support

Understanding the needs of people after cancer treatment in order to support the development of appropriate psychological support and professional practice.

Research Team

The project brings together experts in psychology, psycho-oncology, research methodology and other relevant fields.
